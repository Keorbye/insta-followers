Follow Check

Find who doesn't follow you back on Instagram — right in your browser.

1. Get your Instagram data export

  1. Open the Instagram app or instagram.com and go to Settings → Your activity → Download your information (on web: Settings → Privacy and security → Download your information).
  2. Tap Request a download, select your account, and choose Complete copy (or select only Followers and following).
  3. Set Format to JSON (not HTML) and media quality to low for a faster export.
  4. Submit the request. Instagram emails you a download link — this can take from a few minutes up to 48 hours.
  5. Download the ZIP and extract it. Inside the followers_and_following folder you'll find followers_1.json and following.json.
  6. Upload those two files below. Everything is processed on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
